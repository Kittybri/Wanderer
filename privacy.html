Privacy Policy

Wanderer Bot — Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what data Wanderer Bot ("the Bot") collects, how it is used, and how it is stored. We take your privacy seriously and collect only what is necessary for the Bot to function.

2. Information We Process

Wanderer may process your Discord user ID, display name, channel and server IDs, messages, commands and user-submitted attachments. Persistent features may store conversation memory, preferences, game and achievement state, user-linked relationship and character continuity, reminders, and bounded operational records. Some optional interactions involve shared world and duo data with Wanderer.

When enabled or requested, optional features may process voice audio and transcripts, images, voluntarily enrolled face-recognition profiles, and home/device or computer integration information. You control whether to use optional features and should not send passwords or unrelated private information.

3. How We Use and Share Information

We use information to respond in Discord, operate the features you request, maintain conversational continuity, respect preferences, prevent abuse, and support reliability. Relevant conversation text, images or audio-derived information may be sent to service providers needed for the feature. These can include Discord, Groq (text generation and/or speech transcription), Fish Audio (speech synthesis), optional vision or search services, and the bot's hosting services. Provider processing and retention follow their own terms; the bot does not sell data or use it for targeted advertising.

Information you send in public channels, and responses you request there, may be visible to other server members. Public channel conversations are not a private inbox.

4. Google Connected Accounts (Optional)

If you explicitly connect a Google account, Wanderer can access the verified account identity, Google Calendar events, and Google Tasks required for requested features. This feature requests openid, email, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks. The latter two permissions permit writes; they are not read-only. This connection does not request Gmail or Google Docs/Drive access.

Wanderer can list requested Calendar events and tasks, and propose supported creations and updates. Writes require your separate confirmation. An encrypted Google credential record is linked to your Discord ID in a shared database. Scaramouche and Wanderer have independent access grants; one bot being allowed does not automatically allow the other.

Relevant Calendar or Tasks details may be included in a request to the text-generation provider to answer you. Google credentials are not intended for model prompts. Google information is used only for requested, prominent user-facing features, not for advertising, resale, credit decisions or general-purpose AI model training. Our use of Google Workspace data will adhere to the Google Workspace API User Data and Developer Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Before public launch, the operator must confirm that third-party provider arrangements support these commitments.

4A. Separate Legacy Google Docs Feature

If separately configured by the operator, Wanderer's legacy !fixdoc command can read a Google document that has been shared with its service account (or a configured remote document worker), send document text to a text-generation provider for rewriting, and overwrite the original document. This feature is separate from Google Connected Accounts OAuth and does not use its Calendar/Tasks permissions. It currently requires a dedicated authorization, confirmation, privacy and backup review before being treated as safe for broad use. Do not share sensitive documents with the bot's service account without understanding this behavior.

5. Storage, Security and Retention

Persistent bot records are held in local databases on the hosting environment. Google access and refresh tokens are stored using authenticated encryption with a separate master key. Google linking uses short-lived OAuth sessions, an HTTPS callback and a second in-Discord confirmation. No service can guarantee absolute security.

Conversation, user preferences and continuity records may remain until removed using the bot's privacy controls. Google connection records remain until disconnected, deleted or otherwise invalidated; temporary sessions expire and are cleaned up. Hosting backups, if any, may follow separate retention schedules that the operator must document before public launch. Provider-side retention is controlled by each provider's policies.

6. Your Controls and Deletion

  • !forget <phrase> requests deletion of matching information from supported memory stores.
  • !reset (alias !wipe) starts deletion of user-linked bot data through the bot's privacy reset process.
  • !google permissions controls each bot's Google access; !google disconnect deletes the local connection and both bot grants, and attempts Google token revocation.
  • Use the available in-bot settings to disable optional proactive messages, voice interaction, face enrollment and related features.

If remote Google revocation cannot be verified, also remove the app from your Google Account connections. Deletion in Discord does not remove already delivered public messages, Google events/tasks created at your request, or independent third-party copies. To request assistance, contact the bot operator in the community where you use the bot.

7. Children's Privacy

The bot is not intended for users under 13 or the higher minimum age required by Discord or applicable law.

8. Policy Updates and Contact

Material policy changes should be communicated through the bot's community where feasible. A policy update never grants new Google permissions by itself. For privacy concerns, support or deletion help, contact the bot owner through the Discord community where you use Wanderer. A stable public support contact must be confirmed before Google production publication.
