Scaramouche & Wanderer: Google Connected Accounts

Optional Google Calendar and Google Tasks connections for two independent Discord character bots. This is an unofficial fan project, not affiliated with Google or HoYoverse.

When you request it, the bots can read upcoming Calendar events and Google Tasks. You can also request creation and supported updates of events or tasks, which require an additional explicit confirmation before the operation is sent to Google.

Connect your account

  1. Open /google or use !google connect with either bot in Discord.
  2. Open the private, expiring connection link and approve the Google permissions you want to grant.
  3. Return to the bot's Discord DM and explicitly confirm the link.
  4. Manage separate Scaramouche/Wanderer permissions with !google permissions and remove access with !google disconnect.

Until you approve in Discord, neither bot can use the newly linked account. The other bot never gains access automatically.

Privacy and support

Before linking, read the Google Connected Accounts Privacy Policy. For support or removal requests, contact the bots' maintainer in the Discord community where you use the bots.
