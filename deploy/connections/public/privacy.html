Google Connected Accounts Privacy Policy

Scaramouche & Wanderer Discord bots. Last updated October 2026. The app owner must review this proposed policy against the real hosting, provider contracts and contact details before it is published.

What we access

After your consent, the connection accesses your verified Google account identity (email and provider account ID), Google Calendar events and Google Tasks. It requests openid, email, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks. These Calendar and Tasks scopes permit writes, not just reading. The existing features show events/tasks, create requested items and support certain updates with confirmation; no general task-delete command is offered by the connected bot.

The Connected Accounts feature does not request Gmail, Google Drive, Google Docs or Google Sheets OAuth scopes.

How information is used and shared

The bot retrieves relevant Calendar or Tasks data for the user-requested action. It proposes supported writes and requires separate confirmation. Relevant event and task details may be provided to a text-generation provider when needed to answer your request. Tokens are not intentionally included in model prompts. Information you discuss, including a generated answer using Google data, may also appear in your Discord chat and the bots' ordinary conversation memory, governed by their separate memory/deletion controls.

We do not sell Google account information or use it for advertising. Google and necessary hosting, Discord and AI service providers process information only as needed to deliver the requested features.

Storage and protection

The connection stores your Discord user ID, an obscured identity display and encrypted access and refresh tokens in a shared database. Scaramouche and Wanderer have separate per-bot access grants. The HTTPS callback uses one-time session state and requires explicit confirmation through the requesting bot's Discord message. Short-lived authorization sessions expire.

Retention, disconnection and deletion

Stored connection data remains until you disconnect or request account deletion. Use !google disconnect or the account-management panel to remove local credentials and the bot grants. The service attempts remote token revocation; if Google revocation is not confirmed, you can additionally remove the app in your Google Account's third-party access settings. A bot privacy reset also includes connected-account cleanup. Previously produced conversation messages may still contain data you chose to discuss until you remove them using the bot's conversation privacy controls.

Questions

For support or data removal assistance, contact the bots' maintainer through their Discord community. Return to the Connected Accounts homepage.
